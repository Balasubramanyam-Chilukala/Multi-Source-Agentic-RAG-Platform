Northwind Robotics Benefits Overview

Owner: People Team. This overview applies to permanent employees in France and Germany.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to five unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 10 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Sick leave
Employees should notify their manager before the start of the working day when they are sick. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of sickness.

Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per year for courses, conferences, and books. Unused budget does not carry over.

Health insurance
The company pays 70 percent of the premium for the group health insurance plan with Mutuelle Horizon. Employees may add their partner and children at the same contribution rate.

Pension
Northwind contributes 5 percent of base salary to the company pension plan when the employee contributes at least 3 percent.
