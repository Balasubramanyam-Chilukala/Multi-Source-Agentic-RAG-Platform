# Northwind Robotics Travel Policy (2025)

Effective date: 1 March 2025. This policy replaces the 2022 travel policy in full.
Policy owner: Finance Operations. Questions: travel@northwind-robotics.example.

## Booking flights

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal, Skyline, at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Bookings made fewer than 7 days before departure require a short justification in the portal.

Employees must book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights between six and nine hours. Business class is permitted only for flights longer than nine hours, or for overnight flights where the employee must present to a customer within eight hours of landing, and always requires written approval from a director.

Employees may keep frequent-flyer miles earned on business trips, but miles must never influence the choice of airline when a cheaper equivalent fare exists.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night in most cities. The cap is 250 EUR per night in London, Paris, Zurich, and New York. Stays above the cap require pre-approval from the employee's manager in Skyline before booking.

Employees should choose hotels within 3 kilometres of the office or customer site they are visiting. Room upgrades, minibar charges, and in-room entertainment are personal expenses.

## Ground transport

Public transport is the default within cities. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when travelling between 22:00 and 06:00, when carrying equipment heavier than 10 kg, or when no public transport route takes less than 45 minutes. Rental cars must be the smallest category that fits the travelling party and require manager approval.

Use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre, including fuel, tolls, and wear.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance during travel is 60 EUR, or 75 EUR in London, Paris, Zurich, and New York. Meals included in a hotel rate or conference fee are deducted from the allowance. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Travel insurance and safety

All employees on business travel are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy with GlobalCare. Before travelling to a country rated "high risk" by the security team, employees must complete the travel safety briefing and register their itinerary with the security desk.
