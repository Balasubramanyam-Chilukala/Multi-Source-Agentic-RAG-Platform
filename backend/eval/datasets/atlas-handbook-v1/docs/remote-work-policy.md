# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: People Team. Last reviewed: January 2025.

## Eligibility

All employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. Roles that require lab access, such as hardware test engineers, agree a schedule with their manager instead.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Any stay abroad must be registered with the People Team at least two weeks in advance so that tax and immigration requirements can be checked. Working from countries under international sanctions is never permitted.

## Equipment and allowance

Remote workers receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 EUR for a desk chair, monitor, or similar equipment. Company laptops are provided by IT. Employees also receive a monthly internet allowance of 25 EUR, paid through payroll.

## Security at home

Company devices must not be shared with family members. Employees must lock their screen when leaving the device, and confidential calls must not be taken in public spaces such as cafés or trains.
