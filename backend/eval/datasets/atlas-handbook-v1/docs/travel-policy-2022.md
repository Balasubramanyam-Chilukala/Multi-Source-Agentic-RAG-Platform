# Northwind Robotics Travel Policy (2022) — SUPERSEDED

Status: superseded by the 2025 travel policy on 1 March 2025. Kept for audit purposes only.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights shorter than eight hours. Business class is allowed for flights of eight hours or more with manager approval.

## Hotels

Hotel stays were reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night in all cities, including London.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance was 45 EUR in all locations.

## Private car

Mileage was reimbursed at 0.25 EUR per kilometre.
