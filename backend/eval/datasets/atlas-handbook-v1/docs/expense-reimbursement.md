# Expense Reimbursement Procedure

Owner: Finance Operations. Applies to all employees and contractors with a Northwind Robotics expense account.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses are submitted in the Ledgerly expense app. Every claim must include an itemised receipt; card terminal slips without item details are not accepted. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 90 days are rejected automatically.

Expenses above 500 EUR require approval from the employee's cost-centre owner in addition to their manager.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll run if they are approved before the 20th of the month. Otherwise they are paid with the following payroll run.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times per year can request a corporate card from Finance Operations. Corporate cards must not be used for personal purchases. Lost cards must be reported to the bank and to Finance Operations within 24 hours.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, gym or spa access, and upgrades that are not required by policy.

## Currency

Expenses in foreign currencies are converted using the European Central Bank reference rate on the expense date.
