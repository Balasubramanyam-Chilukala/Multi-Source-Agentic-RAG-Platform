IT Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Robotics. This guide covers your first week with company IT systems.

Your laptop

New employees receive a laptop on their first day from the IT service desk on the second floor of the Lyon office, or by courier for remote hires. Engineers receive a 16-inch laptop with 32 GB of memory; all other roles receive a 14-inch laptop with 16 GB of memory.

Accounts

Your company account is created before your start date. On day one, set up multi-factor authentication and your Vaultline password manager before accessing any other system.

  • Email and calendar: Mailwise
  • Chat: Northwind Chat
  • Code hosting: CodeForge (engineers only)

Getting help

The IT service desk is open from 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time on working days. Outside these hours, urgent issues can be raised by calling extension 4000. Non-urgent requests should be filed as tickets in the Helpline portal.

Returning equipment

When you leave the company, all equipment must be returned within ten working days. IT will send a prepaid shipping box to remote employees.
